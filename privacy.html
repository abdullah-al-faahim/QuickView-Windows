QuickView

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Short version: QuickView collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no accounts, ads, analytics or telemetry. Everything happens on your PC.

What QuickView does on your PC

  • Keyboard. To open a preview when you press Space, QuickView runs a Windows keyboard hook. It looks only at whether the key is Space, Esc, Enter, ← or → and whether File Explorer's file list or the QuickView window is active. It does not record, store or transmit what you type.
  • Files you preview. QuickView reads the selected file only to display it, and only when you ask for a preview. File contents never leave your PC.
  • Office documents. If a document can't be previewed directly, QuickView may ask your installed Microsoft Office or LibreOffice to convert it to PDF. The PDF is cached in your temporary folder and deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Settings. QuickView stores a few preferences (for example, whether it starts with Windows) in your user registry.

Network

QuickView makes no network connections. Some previews are drawn by components installed by Windows or other apps (for example, the PDF or Office preview handlers). Those components follow their own publishers' privacy policies.

Data collection and sharing

QuickView does not collect, store, sell or share personal information, and it contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

Children

QuickView is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes
